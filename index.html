The Popsy story

Two cultures, one cart.

The Popsy Bar was started by a Houston-born brother and sister who wanted to celebrate their Mexican and Pakistani roots through food. That blend still shows up today, from birria and churros to Pakistani signature mocktails.

  • Founded by siblings
  • Mexican & Pakistani flavors
  • Halal menus
